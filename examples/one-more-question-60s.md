# One More Question

还有一个问题想问。约60秒，陪诊人员第一人称英文口播。

## 英文口播

You shouldn't have to leave an appointment thinking, “I wish I'd asked.”

Imagine it's your first hospital visit in China. I'm your companion, and we're going over your questions.

You pause.

“There's one more thing. I'm not sure how to say it.”

“Take your time.”

**[停顿3秒]**

When you're ready, we write it down together. I read it back, and you change a few words.

Now the question says what you mean.

At the appointment, you use that note to ask the doctor. As they answer, you can ask them to go over anything that's unclear.

Before your visit, write down one question you want to make sure you ask. Bring it with you. We'll start there.

## 拍摄说明

- 单人对镜口播，穿插写下问题、读回确认、访客修改笔记的特写。假设场景标注 “Illustrative scenario”。
- 正文115词，加上停顿约60秒，尚未实际试读计时。
