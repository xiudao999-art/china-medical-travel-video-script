# 新题复测：两份未用于修改主稿的简报

## 简报A：预约前的陪游

虚构示意场景，访客在第一次预约前一天有自由时间，明确说喜欢街区日常生活、不喜欢赶景点。可陪同散步、协助看普通餐馆菜单、问路；没有提供城市、餐饮资质、预订渠道或医疗限制。60秒以内，自然温暖，有具体内容。

### A Little Time to Look Around

**Narrator:** Imagine it's the day before your first appointment in China. You have a little time to yourself.

**Visitor:** “I'd love to see everyday life here. I don't want to rush from sight to sight.”

**Companion:** “Then let's walk around the neighborhood. You can choose where we stop.”

**Narrator:** You pause by a fruit stall, watching someone carefully stack the oranges. A few doors along, a restaurant catches your eye.

Your companion helps you read the menu. You pick what sounds good.

Later, you decide to take another street. Your companion asks for directions, and off you go.

Ask about local companionship that follows your interests.

制作备注：假设场景，不冒充真实案例；生活细节可用演员和常见街区场景拍摄；仅提供已知的陪同、看菜单和问路帮助。

## 简报B：不知道怎样向医生提问

给第一次来华就诊的人做实用说明，陪诊可以协助找到诊室、把问题记下来带给医生，未提供医学口译资格。人物自己说“I'm worried I won't know what to ask.”，回应此话，避免文案变成免责声明。60秒以内，无品牌、价格、案例或联系渠道。

### Start with One Question

**Narrator:** Imagine it's your first medical visit in China.

**Visitor:** “I'm worried I won't know what to ask.”

**Companion:** “You don't have to think of everything at once. We can start with one thing you'd like the doctor to explain.”

**Visitor:** “I want to know what happens after today's appointment.”

**Narrator:** Your hospital companion writes that down: “What are the next steps after today?”

You read it and say, “Yes, that's what I mean.”

Then your companion helps you find the consultation room and hands you the note to take to the doctor.

Before your visit, write down one thing you'd like to understand.

制作备注：虚构角色示意；陪诊记录、请本人确认并引路，临床问题留给医生。没有把缺乏口译资质写成大段口播免责声明。
