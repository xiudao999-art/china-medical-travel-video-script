# Three Things to Get Clear Before Your Appointment

## Script

Booked a medical appointment in China? Before you go, get three things clear: your appointment instructions, where you need to go, and what you want to ask.

A hospital companion can check those instructions with you, guide you around the hospital, and write down the questions you choose.

The medical answers come from your clinician.

Start your question list with what matters most to you, so it's there when you need it.
