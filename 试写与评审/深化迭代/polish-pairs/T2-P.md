# A Walk That Follows Your Interests

## Script

Small shops. Handmade things. A street you can explore without checking the time every two minutes.

Picture an afternoon in China, the day before your first appointment. You tell me that's your kind of outing. Rushing between photo spots? No thanks.

So we take a neighborhood walk. You stop at a window full of handmade cups and point to one with a slightly uneven rim.

You want to know whether it was made here. I help you ask the shopkeeper.

There's no need to hurry to the next stop. We can stay, keep walking, or finish here. You choose.

What kind of little shop would make you stop?

## Notes

- 虚构示意情景，开头标注“Illustrative scene”；陶杯是示意道具，不对应真实店铺。
- 以街区步行、停在橱窗前、协助向店主提问呈现陪同；拍摄店铺及人物须取得许可。
- 正文 108 词；按每分钟约 130–135 词并预留停顿，预计 52–58 秒，未经实读计时。
