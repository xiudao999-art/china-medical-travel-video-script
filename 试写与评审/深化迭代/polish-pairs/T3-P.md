# When You Catch the Wrong Turn

## Script

Picture this on a hospital visit in China. I'm your companion, and I've just taken us down the wrong corridor.

This is a fictional example, but here's the part that matters: you notice.

“Doesn't our appointment say the other department?”

I stop. “Thanks for catching that. I took the wrong turn. I'm sorry. Let's check.”

We compare the department on your appointment instructions with the directory. Then I check the route with the information desk.

I come back and show you: “The waiting area is along the other corridor. Shall we head over together?”

We walk there and check the department sign before we sit down.

Your question changed what I did. You didn't have to follow just because I was leading.

When you're getting help with your first visit, “Can we check that?” is always a fair question.

## Notes

- 全篇为虚构示意故事，非真实经历；由陪诊人员单人口播，开场持续显示“Fictional example”，引语以自然语气讲述。
- 可用演员与自制预约指引，在可控走廊布景拍摄核对目录、询问服务台、抵达并核对等候区标识的插入画面；不展示真实患者、病历或未获许可的院内画面。
- 正文139词；按每分钟125–135词，另留约4秒停顿，估计66–71秒，未经实际试读计时。
