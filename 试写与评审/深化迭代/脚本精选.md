这是一份跨轮次精选，非最终 Skill 的单次批量输出。假设场景保留演绎提示；时长是文本估算。产品测试 T5 不代表实际业务。

## T1｜候诊时的沉默

来源：[candidate-A/T1.md](candidate-A/T1.md)。第一轮候选；在新旧匿名比较中获偏好。

### You Can Have a Quiet Minute

#### Script

You don't have to keep a conversation going while you're waiting for your appointment.

As your hospital companion, I can be there without filling every quiet moment.

Say we're sitting together before your first appointment in China. You've stopped talking. I might ask, “Would you like a quiet minute, or help with something?”

You say, “Just a minute to myself.”

“Of course. I'll be right here.”

So we sit. There's no need to explain.

Later, you ask where we go next. We look at your appointment instructions together, match the department name to the sign, and I show you the way.

You can change your mind about what you need. Quiet now, a question later. That's okay.

If you're considering a companion for your visit to China, tell us how you like to spend that waiting time.

#### Notes

- 单人对镜口播，讲述的是假设场景，非真实患者经历；画面可标注“An illustrative scenario”。
- 在“I'll be right here”后留3秒安静；核对指引处可插入无个人信息的道具指引与科室标识特写，在可控布景拍摄。
- 正文137词；按每分钟125–135词，另留约5秒停顿，估计66–71秒，未经实际试读计时。

## T2｜按本人兴趣陪游

来源：[baseline-B/T2.md](baseline-B/T2.md)。基线稿；首轮匿名比较偏好其开头和完整互动，保留较早的好稿。

### Your Kind of Afternoon

#### Script

You don't have to collect landmarks to enjoy a day in China.

Imagine it's the day before your first appointment. You tell me you like little shops, handmade things, and time to look around.

So we take a neighborhood walk. A cup in a shop window catches your eye.

“Was this made here?”

I help you ask the owner, who points to a workbench at the back.

You want to stay and watch. We stay.

When you're ready to move on, I help ask the way to the next street.

That's the point of having company: there's help when you need it, and the afternoon still feels like yours.

Tell us what you like to explore.

#### Notes

- 按面向潜在来华就诊者的竖屏认知阶段短视频创作；这是首次预约前一天的虚构示意，首屏标注 “Illustrative scene”，由演员呈现。
- 用橱窗小杯、店主指向工作台、访客决定停留、陪同者问路四个动作呈现帮助；店铺、店主及拍摄场地需取得许可，无具体城市或品牌承诺。
- 英文正文 116 词，按约 135 词/分钟并留出动作停顿，预计 54–58 秒；未进行真人计时试读。

## T3｜接受纠正与补救

来源：[candidate2/T3.md](candidate2/T3.md)。第二轮候选；在第二轮匿名比较中获偏好。

### When Your Companion Takes a Wrong Turn

#### Script

If I take a wrong turn, you should be able to say so.

Here's an imagined moment from a first hospital visit in China. I'm walking with you toward a waiting area when you stop.

“Doesn't that sign point the other way?”

I look back.

“Sorry—I took us down the wrong corridor. Let's check before we go any farther.”

We return to the directory. I check the appointment instructions against it and ask a staff member to confirm the directions to the waiting area.

Then I show you the directions we've confirmed, and we walk there together. At the entrance, we check the department name again. This time, it matches.

You shouldn't have to worry about offending your companion by speaking up. If something doesn't match what you've been told, point it out. We can stop and check it together.

#### Notes

- 全文为明确虚构的第一人称示意故事；保留口播中的“imagined moment”，画面同步标注“Fictional illustration”。
- 由陪诊者单人口播，可插入布景走廊、示意导视和核对动作；道具不含真实患者资料，实地拍摄需许可。
- 正文 141 词；按每分钟约 130–135 词并留出引语和动作停顿，预计 67–74 秒，未经实读计时。

## T4｜陪诊能提供哪些帮助

来源：[candidate2/T4.md](candidate2/T4.md)。第二轮候选；比较持平，选此稿以优先展示明确定位与确认步骤。

### What an English-Speaking Hospital Companion Can Do

#### Script

On a hospital visit in China, an English-speaking hospital companion can help you find the right room, keep track of your questions, and ask for a clearer explanation.

Before your visit, choose what you most want to ask. We can write those questions down with you and check that we've captured what you mean.

If an explanation is unclear, tell us which part. We can help you ask the clinician to go over that part again.

The clinician provides the medical answer. Speaking English doesn't, by itself, qualify a companion to interpret a medical consultation.

For your next visit, bring a short question list and put your most important question first.

#### Notes

- 单人对镜讲解，不做角色演绎；可举起一张仅写“Questions”的示意便签，配合记录问题的动作。
- 保留医疗问题由医护回答及英语能力不等于医疗口译资质的说明；不增加未确认的语言服务。
- 正文 111 词；按每分钟约 130–135 词并预留停顿，预计 51–57 秒，未经实读计时。

## H1｜小物件与个人意义

来源：[holdout/H1.md](holdout/H1.md)。新题；独立质量评审通过，无旧版配对。

### The Clock That Changed the Walk

#### Script

An old clock can change an afternoon.

Imagine you're walking through a neighborhood in China the day before your appointment. You spot a clock in a shop window.

“My grandfather used to repair clocks,” you say.

Your companion pauses. “Shall I ask if we can look inside?”

You smile. “Yes. Just for a minute.”

Your companion asks the shopkeeper, who waves you in.

You move closer to the clock and start telling your companion about your grandfather. They stay to listen. For a few minutes, the rest of the walk can wait.

What would make you stop on a walk in China?

#### Notes

- 正文共 102 词；按每分钟 120–130 词并留约 4 秒停顿，预计 51–55 秒，未经实际计时朗读。
- 明确设定为预约前一天的假设故事；店主示意入店等为示意情节，不是真实客户证言。拍摄使用演员或获授权参与者，并确认店内拍摄许可。

## H2｜克制而具体的服务介绍

来源：[holdout/H2.md](holdout/H2.md)。新题；独立质量评审通过，无旧版配对。

### Three Things to Get Clear Before Your Appointment

#### Script

Booked a medical appointment in China? Before you go, get three things clear: your appointment instructions, where you need to go, and what you want to ask.

A hospital companion can check those instructions with you, guide you around the hospital, and write down the questions you choose.

The medical answers come from your clinician.

Start your question list with what matters most to you, so it's there when you need it.

#### Notes

- 正文共 72 词；按每分钟 120–130 词并留约 2 秒停顿，预计 35–38 秒，未经实际计时朗读。
- 已删除未经证实的“中国最安全”“费用少 80%”和“有我们就不用担心”，保留已确认的预约指引核对、院内引路及记录本人问题三项协助。

## T5｜虚构产品条件测试

来源：[candidate-A/T5.md](candidate-A/T5.md)。第一轮候选；与基线比较持平。仅用于检验范围与CTA写法，产品条件全部来自虚构测试简报。

### Two Hours of Appointment Support

#### Script

Have an appointment in China, but want someone alongside you for the practical steps?

Our two-hour appointment support covers meeting you, checking appointment instructions, guiding you around the hospital, and writing down your questions. After the appointment, we review the next steps already arranged.

Medical interpreting and city tours aren't included.

To ask about arrangements, enter your city and planned date in the official inquiry form. Availability still needs to be confirmed.

#### Notes

- 仅作虚构产品测试，不是真实企业、真实服务或实际购买邀约；画面全程标注“Fictional product test”。
- 用示意指引、问题便签与无提交功能的表单道具展示服务范围；“官方询问表单存在”仅是本题设定，不添加真实入口或二维码。
- 正文72词；按每分钟120–135词，另留约3秒停顿，估计35–39秒，未经实际试读计时。
