# You Can Be Quiet Here

## Script

You don't have to keep me entertained while you wait.

If you're coming to China for your first medical visit, that's something I'd want you to know about having a hospital companion.

Imagine we're sitting in a waiting area. You've gone quiet. I give you a moment, then ask, “Would you like to check we're in the right place, or just sit for a bit?”

You say, “Let's check first. Then I'd like some quiet.”

So I check the room on your appointment instructions against the waiting-area sign. They match. “Yes, we're in the right place.”

Then I sit with you. There's no need to find another topic.

You can start talking again whenever you want. You can also stay quiet.

To me, companionship includes leaving you room to be yourself.

If that sounds like the support you'd want, ask about accompaniment for your visit.

## Notes

- 单人对镜口播；“Imagine”起为假设场景，并非真实患者故事。屏幕简短标注“Illustrative scenario”。
- “Then I sit with you.”之后留约3秒安静，保持自然表情；若使用指引或标牌道具，只用虚构信息，不拍患者资料。
- 正文145词；按约130–145词/分钟并计入安静停顿，估算约63–72秒，未经真人实测。
