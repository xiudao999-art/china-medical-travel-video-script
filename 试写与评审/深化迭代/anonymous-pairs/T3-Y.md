# When I Take the Wrong Turn

## Script

If I take a wrong turn, I owe you more than an apology.

Here's a fictional example. We're heading to your appointment in China. I lead you down a corridor, and you stop.

“Hang on. Didn't the sign point the other way?”

I stop too. “Let's check.”

We go back to the directory. I compare the department on your appointment instructions with the sign. You're right.

“I took the wrong corridor. I'm sorry. It's this way.”

I point out the matching department name, and we follow the correct direction together. At the waiting area, I check the sign again with you before we sit down.

You shouldn't have to ignore a doubt because I'm the person showing you around. Your question helped us catch a mistake. My job is to listen and put it right.

Ask how appointment support can help you find your way during your visit.

## Notes

- 全文为明确虚构的示意故事，单人口播引用人物原话；画面标注“Fictional example”，不包装成真实经历。
- 可在普通走廊搭建指引牌，补拍核对科室、走向正确等候区、再次核对标牌的动作；只用虚构预约道具。
- 正文148词；按约130–145词/分钟加自然停顿，估算约64–73秒，未经真人实测。
