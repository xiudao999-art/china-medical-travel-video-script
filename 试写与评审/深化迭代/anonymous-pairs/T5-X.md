# A Little Help Finding Your Way

## Script

Have an appointment in China, but want help finding your way?

This two-hour support service includes meeting you, checking your appointment instructions, guiding you through the hospital, and noting your questions. After the visit, we go over the next steps already arranged.

Medical interpreting and city tours aren't included.

To ask about arrangements, enter your city and planned date in the official enquiry form. Availability still needs to be confirmed.
