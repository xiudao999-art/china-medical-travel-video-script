# Two Hours of Appointment Support

## Script

Have an appointment in China, but want someone alongside you for the practical steps?

Our two-hour appointment support covers meeting you, checking appointment instructions, guiding you around the hospital, and writing down your questions. After the appointment, we review the next steps already arranged.

Medical interpreting and city tours aren't included.

To ask about arrangements, enter your city and planned date in the official inquiry form. Availability still needs to be confirmed.
