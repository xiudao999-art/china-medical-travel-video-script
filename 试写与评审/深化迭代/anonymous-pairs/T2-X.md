# A Street Worth Taking Slowly

## Script

Say it's the day before your first appointment in China. You tell me, “I like little shops and handmade things. I don't want to rush around ticking boxes.”

So we walk through a neighborhood. You stop at a window full of handmade cups.

You want to know if they're made here. I help you ask the shopkeeper, who says yes and points to a workbench at the back.

“Can we stay a little longer?” you ask.

Absolutely. The next street can wait.

That's what having someone along can make easier: asking about something that catches your eye, finding your way, and exploring at your pace.

What would make you stop and look?

## Notes

- 虚构示意；开头标注 “Illustrative scene”。用演员拍摄，杯子与工作台为示意道具；店内拍摄及店主出镜须事先获准。
- 用访客停步看杯子、陪同者协助提问、店主指向工作台、两人留下观看串起画面；节奏随访客的选择放慢。
- 正文 112 个英文词；按约 130–140 词／分钟，加转场和观看停顿，预计 52–58 秒，未实测朗读。
