# Getting Your Questions Heard

## Script

An English-speaking companion can help you find the consultation room. They can also help you keep track of the questions you came to ask.

Before your appointment in China, tell them what you want to ask. They can write it down in your words. Read the list together and put your biggest question first.

If an explanation is unclear during the visit, say so. Your companion can help ask the doctor or nurse to explain it again.

Speaking English doesn't mean someone is qualified to interpret medical information. If you need medical interpretation, ask about a qualified interpreter.

Ask what practical support is available for your appointment.
