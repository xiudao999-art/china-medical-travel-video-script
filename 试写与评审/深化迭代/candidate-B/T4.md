# Bring the Question You Want Answered

## Script

An English-speaking hospital companion can help you get to the right room with the questions you want to ask.

Start with what's on your mind. I can help write down your questions, using your words. You choose which one matters most.

At the hospital, I can help you find the consulting room. If an explanation is unclear, I can help ask the clinician to go over it again.

My role here is practical support. Speaking English doesn't qualify me to interpret medical advice. The clinical team provides the medical explanation; ask for a qualified medical interpreter if you need it translated.

Before your appointment, write down one question you don't want to leave unasked.

## Notes

- 单人对镜口播。说到记录问题时，出示仅写有 “My main question” 的示意便签；不插入患者角色或模拟问诊。
- 陪诊范围仅采用简报所给事实；画面不要使用口译资质标识或真实就诊资料。
- 正文 115 个英文词；按约 130–140 词／分钟，加自然停顿，预计 53–59 秒，未实测朗读。
