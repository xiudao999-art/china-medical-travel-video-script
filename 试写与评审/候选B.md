# You Can Take a Moment

## English script

If you go quiet while we’re waiting for your appointment in China, I’d give you a moment.

You might be thinking, translating in your head, or simply enjoying the quiet.

Imagine you keep looking at the queue screen. After a pause, I ask, “Would you like me to check your number?”

You say, “Yes. I’m worried I missed it.”

Now I have something specific to help with. I check with reception, bring you the update, and show you where to look. If I say I’ll watch for your number, I stay and do it.

If you’d rather sit quietly, that’s okay too. You don’t have to keep a conversation going for me.

The same goes for company while you’re travelling here: you get to choose how much conversation you want.

For me, trust starts with small things: listening to your answer, giving you space, and following through.

Planning care in China? Tell me what part of the visit you’d like help with.
