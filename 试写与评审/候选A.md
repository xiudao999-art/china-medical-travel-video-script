# You Can Be Quiet With Me

## English script

If we're waiting for your appointment in China, you don't have to make small talk for my sake.

Imagine you're sitting beside me, looking at the queue screen. You go quiet.

I'd put my phone away and give you a moment.

Then, gently: “Would it help to check what happens next?”

You say, “I'm worried I've missed my turn.”

“Let's check together.”

We ask at reception. They confirm you haven't been called yet, and I show you where your number will appear.

Then you say, “I'd like a few quiet minutes.”

“Of course. I'll sit just over here.”

And I do. You have room to think, and you know where to find me.

That's the kind of support I'd want you to feel: someone who listens to your answer, checks what they can, and follows through.

If you're planning a medical visit to China, ask what appointment support could look like for you.

## 中文制作备注

- 面向考虑来华就医的国际访客，以陪诊人员对镜讲述为主；“Imagine”明确说明这是假设场景。
- 在“give you a moment”后留出3–4秒真实停顿；核实叫号、指示屏幕的动作可用简短示意画面呈现。
- 使用演员及虚构叫号信息；发布前核对实际服务范围，医院实景拍摄需取得许可。
